    What is SIMANFOR

    SIMANFOR is a Decision Support System (DSS) designed to simulate sustainable forest management alternatives and project stand dynamics over time. Developed by the SMART Ecosystems research group at iuFOR (Sustainable Forest Management Research Institute) at the University of Valladolid, the simulator allows managers, researchers, and students to quantitatively assess the impact of different silvicultural prescriptions on forest stands before field application.

    The main platform is freely accessible in the cloud at simanfor.es.

    Forest decision support system

    Managing forest stands requires planning interventions whose biological and economic outcomes unfold over decades. This Decision Support System integrates mathematical models of forest growth, yield, and management to help resolve complex forest planning challenges, providing an objective basis to determine when to intervene, with what intensity, and under which silvicultural criteria.

    SIMANFOR addresses the following operational forest biometrics applications:

    • Stock calculation and natural dynamics: Calculates key stand biometrics from a forest inventory and projects the natural dynamics of the stand.
    • Evaluation of silvicultural alternatives: Compares thinning regimes (systematic, from below, from above, or mixed) against non-intervention baseline scenarios.
    • Quantification of ecosystem services: Estimates timber stocks categorized by commercial log assortments, carbon sequestration balance in aboveground and belowground biomass, and non-timber forest products such as edible mushrooms and fruits.
    • Adaptation to global change: Models the dynamics of mixed stands and evaluates the effects of future climate trajectories on forest stand dynamics.

    Use cases and reference projects

    SIMANFOR has served as the analytical foundation in forest research and knowledge-transfer projects at national and international levels:

    • LifeReb+ Project: Biometric evaluation of management alternatives in uneven-aged stands and coppice conversion of Pyrenean oak (Quercus pyrenaica) in Castile and Leon. Simulation outcomes proved the viability of sustainable quality timber harvesting, published in Forest Systems (Vázquez-Veloso et al., 2025) using the dedicated model Qpyrenaica__liferebollo.
    • COMFOR-SUDOE Project: Assessment of management alternatives and compatibility between timber production, multifunctionality, and conservation in mixed versus pure stands across the SUDOE area. Simulation cases and outputs are accessible in the iuFOR-QuantitativeForestry/COMFOR.NFI repository.
    • CrossForest Project: Large-scale processing of Spanish National Forest Inventory (NFI) plots using semantic technologies and Linked Open Data. From this initiative emerged the ForestExplorer platform, oriented toward exploring and downloading forest plot data for simulation in SIMANFOR (Simulations repository).
    • IMFLEX Project: Analysis of trade-offs among ecosystem services (commercial timber by assortment classes, carbon sequestration, and commercial mushroom yield) along mixture gradients in Pinus sylvestris and Pinus pinaster stands under alternative forest management approaches.

    Differential factors of SIMANFOR

    Internationally, several benchmark forest simulators exist (such as SIMFOR, CAPSIS, Silva, Sortie-ND, FVS). According to Bravo et al. (2025), SIMANFOR differentiates itself through the following core aspects:

    1. Cloud-based service with zero installation: The simulator runs directly within the web browser. It requires no local software installation or specific operating system environments.
    2. Open and free access: The platform is available free of licensing fees for public administrations, forest enterprises, private landowners, researchers, and educational institutions.
    3. Multilingual support: The web platform graphical interface supports multiple languages, while the biometric simulation engine, tabular Excel outputs, and official technical documentation are formally maintained and verified in Spanish and English.
    4. Multi-model and multi-scale architecture: Unlike platforms constrained to a single mathematical formulation, SIMANFOR hosts both individual-tree and stand-level models, dynamic and static, covering diverse species and regions. It is not limited to Spain, including models for other countries and regions such as Norway, the United States, Mexico, Romania, and Ukraine.
    5. Tailored to forest practitioners: The interface design prioritizes operational usability over computational complexity, enabling forestry technicians without programming or modeling background to upload field inventories, design silvicultural schedules, and inspect outputs intuitively.

    Digital ecosystem and connected tools

    SIMANFOR connects seamlessly with open inventory data sources and digital platforms:

    Logo Tool Role and integration with SIMANFOR
    ForestExplorer Linked Open Data portal developed by iuFOR and UVa under the CrossForest project. Allows users to query, filter, and download Spanish National Forest Inventory (NFI) plots in standard formats compatible with SIMANFOR templates.
    FAO Open Foris Arena Global cloud platform by the FAO for forest inventory design, mobile field data collection, and quality control. Enables field-collected plot data to serve directly as input inventories for simulation.

    Integration guides in development

    Detailed protocols for automated plot downloading and inventory preparation from ForestExplorer and FAO Arena will be documented in dedicated guides in upcoming portal updates.

    Call for collaboration

    SIMANFOR is an evolving tool open to collaboration with the forestry, scientific, and educational community. We welcome professionals and institutions to participate in the following areas:

    • Integration of new forest models: If you have developed or calibrated equations for growth, mortality, regeneration, or any other biometric variable of interest, we can assist in integrating them into the simulator to provide visibility and access to the community.
    • Research projects: Partnerships to incorporate specific modules for ecosystem services not yet covered, new models or calculation variants, or to develop new software functionalities.
    • Teaching and technical training: Pedagogical support for using SIMANFOR in university courses (Silviculture, Forest Management, Forest Biometrics), Bachelor's or Master's thesis supervision, and workshops for practitioners.

    To propose a collaboration or incorporate a forest model into the platform, contact us at simanfor.forest@uva.es (or reach out directly to the collaborators).

    Citation of individual forest models

    When reporting simulations, it is recommended to also cite the specific growth and yield model used, as simulation results are underpinned by the individual forest growth model selected. Detailed references for each model are provided in their respective technical documentation pages within the Models section.
