    Forest dimensions and growth metrics

    Fundamental mensuration metrics measured and calculated at tree level (diameter at breast height, height, crown dimensions) and stand level (basal area, quadratic mean diameter, dominant height).

    Plot-level variables

    • Age (T) — decimal  •  years

      Mean age of the forest stand in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: edad, t, stand_age

    • Density (N) — decimal  •  trees/ha  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Number of trees per hectare.

      Accepted names in inventory: n, pies_ha, densidad, density

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (Density_SP1, Density_SP2, Density_SP3).

    • Species {num} density proportion (N_prop) — decimal  •  ratio  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Proportion of trees of species {num} relative to total stand density.

      Accepted names in inventory: n_prop, n_proportion, proporcion_n, {sp_lower}_n_prop, {sp_lower}_n_proportion, n_prop_{sp_lower}

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (Species SP1 density proportion, Species SP2 density proportion, Species SP3 density proportion).

    • quadratic_mean_diameter (dg) — decimal  •  cm  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Quadratic mean diameter of the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: diametro_medio_cuadratico

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (quadratic_mean_diameter_SP1, quadratic_mean_diameter_SP2, quadratic_mean_diameter_SP3).

    • mean_diameter_at_breast_height (d_mean) — decimal  •  cm  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Arithmetic mean diameter at breast height in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: diametro_normal_medio

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (mean_diameter_at_breast_height_SP1, mean_diameter_at_breast_height_SP2, mean_diameter_at_breast_height_SP3).

    • Basal_area (G) — decimal  •  m2/ha  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Cross-sectional area of tree stems at breast height of all the trees within the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: g, g_ha, area_basimetrica, basal_area

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (Basal_area_SP1, Basal_area_SP2, Basal_area_SP3).

    • Species {num} basal area proportion (G_prop) — decimal  •  ratio  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Proportion of basal area of species {num} relative to total stand basal area.

      Accepted names in inventory: g_prop, g_proportion, proporcion_g, {sp_lower}_g_prop, {sp_lower}_g_proportion, g_prop_{sp_lower}

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (Species SP1 basal area proportion, Species SP2 basal area proportion, Species SP3 basal area proportion).

    • Species {num} proportion (prop) — decimal  •  ratio  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2

      Proportion of species {num} in mixed stands.

      Accepted names in inventory: prop, proportion, proporcion, {sp_lower}_prop, {sp_lower}_proportion, prop_{sp_lower}

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (Species SP1 proportion, Species SP2 proportion).

    • Dominant_diameter (Do) — decimal  •  cm  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Dominant diameter, calculated as the mean diameter of the 100 thickest trees per hectare.

      Accepted names in inventory: Diametro_dominante

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (Dominant_diameter_SP1, Dominant_diameter_SP2, Dominant_diameter_SP3).

    • Dominant_section (So) — decimal  •  cm  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Mean cross-sectional area of dominant trees in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: Seccion_dominante

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (Dominant_section_SP1, Dominant_section_SP2, Dominant_section_SP3).

    • mean_height (h_mean) — decimal  •  m  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Arithmetic mean tree height in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: altura_media

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (mean_height_SP1, mean_height_SP2, mean_height_SP3).

    • Dominant_height (Ho) — decimal  •  m  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Dominant stand height (mean height of the 100 largest trees per hectare).

      Accepted names in inventory: Altura_dominante

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (Dominant_height_SP1, Dominant_height_SP2, Dominant_height_SP3).

    • minimum_basal_area (g_min) — decimal  •  cm2  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Minimum individual tree basal area in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: area_basimetrica_minima

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (minimum_basal_area_SP1, minimum_basal_area_SP2, minimum_basal_area_SP3).

    • mean_basal_area (g_mean) — decimal  •  cm2  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Mean individual tree basal area in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: area_basimetrica_media

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (mean_basal_area_SP1, mean_basal_area_SP2, mean_basal_area_SP3).

    • maximum_basal_area (g_max) — decimal  •  cm2  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Maximum individual tree basal area in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: area_basimetrica_maxima

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (maximum_basal_area_SP1, maximum_basal_area_SP2, maximum_basal_area_SP3).

    • minimum_diameter_at_breast_height (d_min) — decimal  •  cm  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Minimum diameter at breast height in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: diametro_normal_minimo

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (minimum_diameter_at_breast_height_SP1, minimum_diameter_at_breast_height_SP2, minimum_diameter_at_breast_height_SP3).

    • maximum_diameter_at_breast_height (d_max) — decimal  •  cm  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Maximum diameter at breast height in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: diametro_normal_maximo

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (maximum_diameter_at_breast_height_SP1, maximum_diameter_at_breast_height_SP2, maximum_diameter_at_breast_height_SP3).

    • minimum_height — decimal  •  m  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Minimum total tree height in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: altura_minima

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (minimum_height_SP1, minimum_height_SP2, minimum_height_SP3).

    • maximum_height — decimal  •  m  •  multi-species: sp1, sp2, sp3

      Maximum total tree height in the plot.

      Accepted names in inventory: altura_maxima

      Simulation breakdown: This variable is automatically expanded for each target stand species (maximum_height_SP1, maximum_height_SP2, maximum_height_SP3).

    Tree-level variables

    • age (t) — decimal  •  years

      Age of the individual tree.

      Accepted names in inventory: tree_age, edad, t

    • expansion_factor — decimal  •  dimensionless

      Tree expansion factor. Represents the number of trees per hectare that a measured tree represents.

      Accepted names in inventory: expan, factor_expansion, expansion_factor

    • dbh_1 (d_1) — decimal  •  cm

      First cross caliper measurement of diameter at breast height. Used to measure diameter at breast height in a cross shape.

      Accepted names in inventory: dap_1

    • dbh_2 (d_2) — decimal  •  cm

      Second perpendicular cross caliper measurement of diameter at breast height. Used to measure diameter at breast height in a cross shape.

      Accepted names in inventory: dap_2

    • dbh_over_cork (d_oc) — decimal  •  cm

      Diameter at breast height measured over the cork layer (Quercus suber).

      Accepted names in inventory: dap_sobre_corcho

    • dbh (d) — decimal  •  cm

      Diameter at breast height of the tree (measured at 1.30 meters).

      Accepted names in inventory: d, diametro, diametro_normal, d_normal, diameter, dn, dap

    • bark_1 — decimal  •  mm

      First measurement of bark thickness at breast height (1.30 m). Used to measure bark thickness in a cross shape.

      Accepted names in inventory: corteza_1

    • bark_2 — decimal  •  mm

      Second measurement of bark thickness at breast height (1.30 m). Used to measure bark thickness in a cross shape.

      Accepted names in inventory: corteza_2

    • bark — decimal  •  mm

      Mean tree bark thickness measured at 1.30 m.

      Accepted names in inventory: corteza

    • basal_area (g) — decimal  •  cm2

      Cross-sectional trunk area at 1.30 m height.

      Accepted names in inventory: area_basimetrica

    • basal_area_intraspecific (g_intrasp) — decimal  •  m2/ha

      Basal area of trees of the same species as the subject tree, expressed as basal area per hectare.

      Accepted names in inventory: area_basimetrica_intraespecifica

    • basal_area_interspecific (g_intersp) — decimal  •  m2/ha

      Basal area of trees of other species than the subject tree, expressed as basal area per hectare.

      Accepted names in inventory: area_basimetrica_interespecifica

    • basal_area_per_hectare (g_ha) — decimal  •  m2/ha

      Subject tree basal area expressed per hectare (considering the tree expansion factor).

      Accepted names in inventory: area_basimetrica_por_hectarea

    • normal_circumference (c) — decimal  •  cm

      Normal trunk circumference measured at 1.30 m from ground level.

      Accepted names in inventory: circunferencia_normal

    • stump_height (h_stump) — decimal  •  m

      Stump height after tree felling.

      Accepted names in inventory: altura_tocon

    • total_height (h) — decimal  •  m

      Total tree height from ground level to top apex.

      Accepted names in inventory: h, altura, height, altura_total
